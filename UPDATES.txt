## 2026-10-12-mx-cradle
- Rig is a compact MX cradle: 1.17 x 1.21 m footprint, load plate 0.56 m off the floor
- Lean goes to 44 deg and pitch to 41 deg, past what the old tall deck could reach
- Surge, sway, and yaw run on real rails sized to their travel, so the deck moves as far as the cue asks
- Each actuator is a fixed barrel and sliding rod on a trunnion foot, with the drive cabinet behind the frame
- 49 individual bike parts in the right panel instead of 18 material groups
- Rider is rebuilt from rounded body forms, no more blocky torso
